Privacy Policy

Lumi Words: Word Ladder Puzzle · NexPlay Studios Ltd · Effective 22 November 2026

This privacy policy explains how Lumi Words: Word Ladder Puzzle (the "game"), published by NexPlay Studios Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales ("we", "us"), handles information about you. We are the data controller for the game.

The short version

We do not ask for your name, email, contacts, photos or location, and the game has no accounts. Your progress is saved only on your device. The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob, and it measures how the game is played with Google Analytics for Firebase. Google collects some device data for these purposes, as described below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, stars, collected picture pairs, spare steps, daily streaks, the list of words you have played and your sound, music, background and vibration settings in the app's local storage on your device. This information is never sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the game.

Sharing a result

If you tap Share result, the game makes a picture and a short text of your finished word ladder and hands them to your device's own share menu. They go only to the app you then choose, and nothing is sent to us.

Play statistics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase, provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, to understand how the game is played so that we can improve it. Where it is switched on, it records events such as a puzzle being started, finished or abandoned, the word length and difficulty chosen, the number of steps, hints and undos used, and whether the word list or the share button was used. These events are linked to an app-instance identifier and, where available, your Android advertising ID, together with general device information (model, Android version, language, country estimated from your IP address). They do not include your name, email address or the words you type.

UK, EEA and Switzerland: play statistics are switched off unless you allow them in Google's consent form, shown the first time you open the game. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Elsewhere: play statistics are on by default.

Analytics data is kept by Google for 2 months and then deleted. You can read how Google handles it at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

We use the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob), provided by Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC, to show ads. When an ad is requested or shown, the SDK automatically collects and shares the following with Google:

DataWhat it is
Device and account identifiersYour Android advertising ID, app set ID, and, where applicable, other identifiers related to accounts signed in on the device.
IP addressYour device's IP address, which may be used to estimate its general (approximate) location.
Product interactionsSuch as app launches, taps and ad video views.
Diagnostic informationSuch as crash logs, app launch time, hang rate and energy usage.

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. It is encrypted in transit (TLS). We do not receive it in a form that identifies you, and we do not sell it.

You can learn how Google uses this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and read Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Your choices about ads

  • UK, EEA and Switzerland: the first time you open the game you are shown Google's consent form, which lets you accept or reject personalised ads and other uses of your data. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options.
  • Everywhere: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device's Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads). If you opt out, you may still see non-personalised ads.
  • Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Legal bases (UK GDPR / EU GDPR)

Where personalised advertising or play statistics require consent, we rely on your consent, collected through Google's consent form. Outside the UK, EEA and Switzerland we rely on our legitimate interests in improving the game for play statistics. For non-personalised ads, ad measurement, and fraud prevention, we rely on our legitimate interests in funding a free game and keeping it secure.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

Retention and deletion

We do not keep any personal data on our own servers. Data stored on your device stays there until you clear the app's storage or uninstall the game. Play statistics in Google Analytics for Firebase are deleted after 2 months. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept and deleted according to Google's own retention policies; you can also reset your advertising ID at any time as described above.

Security

The game does not transmit your saved progress anywhere. Data sent by the Google Mobile Ads SDK and by Google Analytics for Firebase is encrypted in transit using TLS. The game requests no sensitive device permissions (no camera, microphone, contacts, files or location).

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data about you ourselves, most requests are best handled through the controls above or directly with Google. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) or your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and for significant changes we will let players know inside the game.
