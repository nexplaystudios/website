Our mission

Make the game you reach for in a spare minute, and make it fair.

Most people play on a phone, a few minutes at a time. We build for those minutes: games that are clear in the first ten seconds, satisfying in the first level, and worth opening again tomorrow.

One idea per game

Each game does one thing and tells you exactly what to do. When testers found a second goal confusing, we took it out.

Adverts that play fair

No banners. Never an advert in the middle of a level. Reward adverts only appear behind a button you choose to press.

A reason to come back

A daily puzzle that is the same for every player, streaks, gifts and a growing album of pictures to collect.

How we build

A small studio built around AI from day one

NexPlay is AI-native from day one. Our games are written with AI coding agents and directed, play-tested and signed off by people. That lets a very small team test many ideas and put its time behind the ones players keep.

  1. 01

    Start from the charts

    Every game begins with a fresh look at what the stores show people are downloading in its genre. We match what players expect, then add one twist of our own.

  2. 02

    Build on one shared base

    Menus, daily puzzles, albums, adverts, consent and store graphics are shared between games, so a new title is mostly its new idea. Levels are generated and checked by a solver before release.

  3. 03

    Play it on a real phone

    Nothing ships until it has been played on a phone and changed in response. What we learn from real play shapes every release.

SEIS

For the earliest money into a company.

  • 50% income tax relief on the amount invested
  • Relief on up to £200,000 invested per tax year
  • No capital gains tax on a profit from the shares after three years
  • Loss relief if the shares are sold at a loss
  • A company can raise up to £250,000 in total under SEIS

EIS

For the growth funding that follows.

  • 30% income tax relief on the amount invested
  • Relief on up to £1,000,000 invested per tax year
  • No capital gains tax on a profit from the shares after three years
  • Other capital gains can be deferred by reinvesting them
  • Loss relief if the shares are sold at a loss

Important. Investing in early-stage companies is high risk: you could lose all the money you invest, and shares in a private company are hard to sell. Tax reliefs depend on your personal circumstances, on the shares being held for at least three years and on the company continuing to qualify, and the rules can change. Advance assurance is HMRC's view that a share issue is likely to qualify; it does not guarantee that relief will be given.
